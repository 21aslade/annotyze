What is the title of your project?
annotyze

Write a brief abstract (summary) of your project (125 words or less). What are you going to do? 
annotyze analyzes information about your scripture annotations, giving you insights into your study habits and the scriptures at the same time

Specifically, what are you going to accomplish or produce from this project? 
I'm going to make a program (hosted on Github) that takes in an annotation file from the Church website and provides analytics and information about your annotations

What are your specific plans to accomplish this project?
(List step-by-step what you plan to do.)
I plan to develop the program in several phases
1. I'll set up infrastructure for parsing annotation files
2. I'll create a static site (using Leptos) to display the information
3. I'll run analytics on the information and display useful information.
	Planned analytics (I may not get everything):
		- Book/chapter annotation count breakdown
		- Graph of annotation location over time
		- Graph of annotation frequency across time
		- Graph of tag frequency across time
4. I'll host the site on Github and sumit a URL

Why did you choose to do this project, and how will it be meaningful to you? 
I've always wanted to have a tool like this; it's useful for reflecting on your study habits and also organizing your notes.

How will your project accomplish the four course outcomes?
(Write one sentence for each outcome below.)

Nurture your faith in Jesus Christ and deepen your commitment to enduring Christian discipleship.
	Reflecting on the history of my discipleship reminds me of things I've forgotten and prepares me for deeper discipleship in the future
Improve your knowledge and understanding of Book of Mormon doctrines in your contexts and apply those doctrines to your own lives in meaningful and appropriate ways.
	Looking over my annotations will remind me of truths and help me see where the most important truths are. The enhancement of my study will make potential applications clearer.
Increase scriptural literacy and develop skills of lifelong scripture study, including considerations of context and intent, and principles of sound exegesis.
	Seeing how my annotations change over time will make it clearer what I need to do to improve my scriptural literacy and keep up good study habits long-term.

	Over time, I've collected a lot of notes and annotations that had personal applications attached to them. I've forgotten all of them, but it's difficult to effectively review them with the Church's tools. This tool will help me understand them better.
